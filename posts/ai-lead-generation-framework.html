Lead gen

A simple framework for AI-powered lead generation

By Sandeep Tharakaram10 Oct 20264 min read

AI makes lead gen faster, not smarter — unless you give it a framework. Here's a simple one that keeps quality high.

Example article — replace with your own. The layout and SEO tags are ready.

1. Define the ICP precisely

Who, exactly, gets real value from what you sell? The tighter the definition, the better everything downstream works.

2. Find signals, not just companies

Target buying signals — hiring, funding, tech adoption — not a static list.

3. Enrich and score

Use AI to enrich each lead and rank them, so you spend time on the best fits first.

4. Personalize at the right layer

Personalize the opening and the offer — not every sentence. Relevance beats volume.

5. Track and learn

Measure reply and conversion rates per segment, and double down on what works.
