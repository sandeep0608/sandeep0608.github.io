5 marketing tasks to automate with n8n

By Sandeep Tharakaram15 Oct 20264 min read

Most marketing teams lose hours a week to work a simple workflow could handle. Here are five good places to start with n8n.

1. Lead capture to CRM and Sheets

Route every new form submission into your sheet, CRM, and a Slack ping — automatically.

2. Social scheduling from a content sheet

Draft once in a sheet; let a workflow publish on schedule across channels.

3. Weekly performance digests

Pull numbers from your analytics tools into a tidy weekly email.

4. Review and mention monitoring

Get alerted when your brand is mentioned, so you can respond fast.

5. Lead enrichment

Enrich new leads with company data before they hit your pipeline.
